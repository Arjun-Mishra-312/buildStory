# Buildstory image concept prompts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-08-07. These are
selection concepts, not yet optimized production assets.

## Favicon concepts

All favicon prompts requested a centered, text-free, high-contrast PNG mark
that remains legible at 16–32 px, using the Buildstory palette (`#0c1018`,
`#f6f0e3`, `#f36f56`, and optionally `#6f86e8`) with no mockup or watermark.

1. **Receipt Fold** — A folded receipt whose negative space suggests a
   lowercase “b,” with a coral fold/tear accent.
2. **Story Spine** — Two offset page slabs joined by a coral rule, suggesting
   a story becoming a shipped artifact.
3. **Commit Spark** — Four chunky bracket-like wedges around a cobalt node,
   with one coral wedge.
4. **Archive Seal** — A circular editorial seal interrupted by a coral story
   line and a punched receipt notch.
5. **Build Trail** — Three rising commit blocks ending in a coral check-like
   corner.

## Card background concepts

All card prompts requested a text-free, dark editorial 4:5 base image with a
large low-detail region for live cream typography, compatibility with a wide
center crop, and no UI, readable text, logo, people, device, or watermark.

- **Repository Topography** — Layered contour lines and branching commit paths
  on midnight paper, with one coral route and sparse cobalt nodes.
- **Redacted Receipt** — A tactile collage of torn/perforated receipt fragments,
  faint ruled columns, and unreadable redaction bars around a dark center.
- **Decision Blocks** — Bauhaus-like clipped modules, connector rules, and a
  punched seal assembled mainly along the lower-right edge.
